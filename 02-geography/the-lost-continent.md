# The Lost Continent

**A landmass that the Doom took and never gave back.** It lies beneath the sea today — a vast plateau shelving up to within a few hundred metres of the surface, and in places far less.

## Why it exists necessarily

This is the part worth holding onto: **the lost continent is not an invented myth. It is a deduction.**

The world is Earth. The Doom drowned land. It follows that some of Earth's original landmass never came back up. Anyone in this world who thinks clearly about the Doom for long enough arrives at the lost continent as a *conclusion*, not a legend — which is exactly the opposite of how Atlantis works in our world, and far more interesting.

It also means the evidence is real and available:

- **Soundings.** Any ship with a lead line and patience finds the plateau. The shallows are simply *there*, in the middle of an ocean, hundreds of miles across. They are on charts. Sailors know them as fishing grounds and hazards.
- **Dredge hauls.** Worked stone. Metal. Things that are not rock.
- **The arithmetic.** Compare the land that should exist against the land that does. It does not balance.

## What that gives you

**Theories, factions and cranks.** With no dominant church and no consensus on whether the Doom is even over, the lost continent is an open scholarly question that anyone may hold an opinion about. There will be:

- scholars who have mapped the plateau's edge and argue about its extent
- a faith or three for whom it is the drowned holy land, the punished place, or the world's true centre
- treasure interests who dredge it — because unlike surface ruins, which were scavenged bare over fifteen centuries, **the drowned world was never picked over**
- the respectable position that it is simply seabed and the whole business is nonsense
- at least one state whose claim to greatness rests on being its supposed heir

**A reason deep-water capability is strategic.** In a world of trade and chokepoints, whoever can work at depth can reach something nobody else can. That is a technology race with a clear prize.

**Intrigue that does not require anyone to be lying.** The best kind. Everyone has partial evidence; nobody has the whole; every position is defensible; and the truth is genuinely down there.

## Relationship to the world's other secrets

Deliberately kept separate from the Elsring for now — two hidden things that turn out to be the same hidden thing is a cheaper trick than two that are genuinely different. The lost continent is **public mystery**: argued over in journals and taverns. The Elsring are **private truth**: nobody suspects anything at all.

That contrast is useful. The world is loudly obsessed with a puzzle it cannot solve, and completely blind to the one living under its feet. That is the Fallacy of Fear rendered as geography — energy poured into a safe mystery, none at all into the dangerous one.

## Open

- How much of the former landmass is down there? A tenth? A third?
- Is any of it *nearly* surfaced — reefs, shoals, an island that is the peak of a drowned range and is therefore the only piece anyone can stand on? (Strongly recommended. That island is a place.)
- Was it inhabited before the Doom, and were there survivors from it? Refugees from a drowning continent would be a founding population somewhere.
- Does anyone dive it, and how deep can this world's technology reach?
- Which of the hundred-odd realms claims descent from it, and is the claim any good?
